# 🏕️ Survivor Camp: Quick Start
Survivor Camp is the pixel-art game at the bottom of the screen during Sharpie360's adventures. Type `!camp join` in stream chat and your own little survivor walks into camp. Jest the cat hangs out too. 🐈

**Three Ways To Play**
- **`!` commands go in STREAM chat** (YouTube, Rumble, Twitch, Kick, VP Zone)
- **`/` commands go in DISCORD**
- **The camp site:** camp.sharpiethreesixty.media. Sign in with Discord or Google to join, run actions, open your backpack and visit the shop.
Trick: the link code is born in Discord (or on the camp site), used in stream chat.

**Stream Chat (`!`)**
`!camp join` / `!camp leave` walk in or out
`!camp dance` `wave` `spin` `dance2` `cheer` emotes (while in camp)
`!camp left` `right` `stop` `goto 0-100` walk around
`!camp sit` `watch` `getup` sit down, watch the adventure, stand up
`!camp hand left|right|swap|empty` pick the hand that holds your tool
`!camp scrap WORD` grab a stream code when one pops up
`!camp link CODE` / `!camp unlink` link this chat name
`!camp help` list commands

**Link Your Account (Once Per Platform)**
1. In Discord type `/camp link` (or press **Get Link Code** on the camp site). You get a code (one use, 10 min).
2. Paste the whole `!camp link CODE` line in STREAM chat while we're live. Not in Discord!
3. Your name turns gold and SharpieBot DMs you. Done. No passwords, ever.

**Why Link?**
Gold name, no 3-minute guest limit (you stay until 10 min without chatting), your own look and outfit, watch-time Scrap and stream codes, a one-time **100 Scrap welcome bonus**, and free **Starter Picks** (2 hats, 2 outfits, 2 tools from the starter items, in `/camp backpack`). Signed in on the camp site but no chat name linked? You can still join camp from the site or Discord.

**Earning Scrap** (earned Scrap is also XP)
`/camp daily` or the site's Daily button: 16 on day 1, +1 per day in a row, up to 25
Discord chat: 2 per message (one a minute), up to 120/day
Voice chat: 1 per 5 min (2+ people), up to 60/day
Watching live (linked): 1 per 5 min, up to 36/day
Stream codes (linked): usually 20 each, normal codes up to 100, 100/day cap. **Special Drops** pay up to 500 and skip the cap.
Rank-ups pay bonus Scrap too: 25 for Scavenger, +25 per rank, up to 225 for Apex.

**Ranks** (by lifetime XP)
Scavenger 60 · Forager 200 · Nomad 500 · Pathfinder 1,100 · Ranger 2,000 · Survivalist 3,750 · Legend 6,250 · Paragon 10,000 · Apex 15,000
