# 📻 Nature Radio, with DJ NatureDJ
Nature Radio is the chill station in our nature voice channel, and its DJ is **NatureDJ**. Hop in and the DJ spins every track in order, on repeat, or loops just your favorite. You run everything with `/naturedj` (the old `/natureradio` commands are gone).

**On The Playlist**
`dunes`
`storms`
`waves`
`woods`

**Ask The DJ (Discord, `/`)**
`/naturedj tracks` list the tracks (▶️ marks the one playing, plus the loop mode)
`/naturedj play track:` switch tracks (start typing, it autocompletes). In loop all, the playlist carries on from there
`/naturedj pause` pause it for everyone in the channel
`/naturedj resume` bring it back
`/naturedj now` what's playing, how far in, the loop mode, what's up next and which DJ is on the decks
`/naturedj loop mode:all` every track in order, then back to the start
`/naturedj loop mode:one` repeat one track (add `track:`, or pick from the menu)
`/naturedj skip direction:next` next track (`previous` goes back). It wraps around, and in loop one it changes the looped track
`/naturedj jump direction:forward` jump 5 minutes ahead in the track (`back` goes 5 minutes back)
`/naturedj volume level:` set the volume for everyone, 1-100 (leave it empty to see the current volume)

**Meet The DJs**
Three DJs take turns on the decks, and every **2 hours** the DJ's picture, banner, bio and server nickname change: 🥥 **DJ Coco Vinne** -> 🌅 **DJ Sunnie Dee** -> 🌊 **DJ Misty Sea**, then round again.

**Who Can Ask**
Anyone sitting in the channel can play, loop, skip, jump, change the volume, pause or resume. No linking needed.

**Cooldowns**
After you use play, loop, skip, jump, volume, pause or resume, wait **30 seconds** before using one again. Everybody shares a **5 second** cooldown too, so there's no ping-ponging. If you're too fast it tells you how long to wait.

**Empty Channel**
When nobody's in the channel for **1 minute**, the DJ takes a break and pauses. It starts again the moment someone joins. It stays in the channel the whole time.

**Staff Only**
`/naturedj join` bring the DJ in
`/naturedj leave` send the DJ home (it stays out until `/naturedj join`)

Stay Sharp! 🌊
